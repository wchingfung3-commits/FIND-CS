# First-company research pilot

Open /#/pilot on the Preview deployment. Choose a company and an official service
category to see source-linked contact candidates. This is separate from the mock
home catalog and from published live database records.

Acceptance: check all five company tabs; issue-specific channels should be distinct
from general fallback candidates. DHL WhatsApp must have no direct contact button.
Hang Seng official # → 9 instruction must only appear for suspected-message
reporting, with an untested label. Never treat it as a general human shortcut.
No company may claim a complete menu, measured fastest channel or human-reaching
verification. Officer hours must not be confused with 24-hour automated systems.

Next validation: pilot an ordinary general query, observe language and problem
branches, note every transition and handoff, and stop at account/order verification.
Do not invent account details, lost cards or fraud cases. Persist actual test time,
channel, issue, route revision, observed outcome and reproducible evidence only
once that interaction exists. Record blocked/login-only branches rather than
marking them successful. Check that a human handles the selected issue, not merely
that a call was answered. WhatsApp automation and paid calls remain integration
and authorization gates, not enabled by this research preview.

The pilot is a bounded research starting point, not all publicly documented
specialist hotlines or all menu branches. Sources and remaining gaps are visible.
The full company inventory, current menus and real-time queues require observation.
